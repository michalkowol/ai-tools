Inbox: flag two-minute tasks clearly

Processing in /daily-workflow:inbox judges every entry against the two-minute rule, and the page clearly shows the entries that meet it, together with the concrete action to do right away.

Context

The GTD two-minute rule: if something takes less than two minutes, do it right away instead of planning it. Processing already has the done outcome with this definition, but the page does not say it explicitly: an entry with Done looks like any other and there is no information about what exactly to do. The user wants clear information that an entry meets the rule.

Facts

  • Outcome done: "takes under two minutes and the user can do it right now (a short reply, an approval)", Status Done (today). plugins/daily-workflow/skills/inbox/references/processing.md, Proposal rules
  • An entry has one why line; the page shows the meta line, "AI proposal", the original with its source, the title, the fields, "+ Add card", why and the note. scripts/wl/assets/inbox-components.js renderEntry()
  • Entry order: waiting_on_me, then the due date of the first card, then oldest first. scripts/wl/inbox.py order()
  • The e key accepts the proposal and moves on. inbox-components.js, shortcuts

Out of scope

  • The skill doing the action itself (e.g. sending a Slack reply).
  • Capture and the day plan page.

Decision log

How does the page show an entry that meets the two-minute rule?

An olive box under the fields: the action to do, a button that opens the source (Slack, PR, email) and "Done, next (e)". The most visible and leads straight to the action; takes the most space. An olive bar under the meta line: "2-minute rule · do it now: …, then press e". Seen first, but no button to the source. A clay "⏱ under 2 min" pill next to the title and a "Do it now: …" line under it. Little space, but easier to miss. The left edge of the card is olive, the eyebrow says "AI proposal · 2-minute task", a "→ …" line under the title. The most subtle.

Recommended option accepted. The box is the clearest signal and puts the source and the next step one click away.

What does the AI record when judging the two-minute rule?

A new entry field two_minute: one sentence saying exactly what to do (e.g. "Reply 'yes, keep the optional field' in the DM"), empty when the rule does not fit. The page shows it in the chosen look. A two_minute: true/false field; the action follows from the title and the reason. Less text, but less clear what to do. The rule = a done proposal, the page highlights every entry with Done. No JSON change, but a Done set by hand gets highlighted too.

Recommended option accepted. The box in D1 needs the concrete action to be useful.

Which status does the AI propose for an entry that meets the rule?

As today (done = "under two minutes, do it right now"): you do it while processing and move on. Processing stays fast, and you do the two-minute tasks right after it from today's list.

Recommended option accepted. The rule means doing it during processing.

Do entries that meet the two-minute rule go first?

Someone waiting on me first, then the due date, then the oldest. Two-minute tasks are often in the first group anyway. Quick wins right away, the Inbox shrinks faster; longer entries that block others move down.

Quick wins first. Inside both groups the current order stays (waiting on me, due date, oldest).

Where else are two-minute tasks visible?

E.g. "35 entries · 6 under 2 min" next to the date and the capture counters. Segments of two-minute entries have an olive dot, so it is visible where they are. A ⏱ sign next to entries with the rule in the table at the end.

Recommended options accepted: the user sees how many quick wins there are and where they are.
