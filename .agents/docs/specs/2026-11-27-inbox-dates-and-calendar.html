Inbox: calendar date picker, the Calendar status and Reminder equal to Due Date

Three fixes to the Inbox page: no Reminder on the day of the Due Date; the Calendar status (plus Today and Today candidates) can be picked; dates are picked from a calendar, past dates included.

Context

Three remarks from the user about the Inbox page of /daily-workflow:inbox:

  1. When the Due Date and the Reminder are on the same day, setting the Reminder makes no sense.
  2. The Calendar status cannot be picked.
  3. Dates should be picked from a calendar, past dates included.

Facts

  • Page statuses: done, next, waiting, agenda, someday, icebox, trash, keep; the fields per status are in FIELDS. plugins/daily-workflow/scripts/wl/assets/inbox-logic.js OUTCOMES, FIELDS
  • Reminder and Due Date are a <select> from dateOptions(): the empty value, today and 14 days (Due Date: 30), plus the current value if it is outside the list. inbox-logic.js dateOptions, inbox-components.js renderFields
  • Switching to Waiting for without a Reminder sets the Reminder to today + 3 days. inbox-logic.js setField
  • AI rule: Reminder never in the past; Due Date only for a real deadline. skills/inbox/references/processing.md
  • Apply: an empty reminder / due clears the date; a missing field stays unchanged. processing.md, Apply
  • Top view: Reminder ≤ today or Due Date ≤ tomorrow. skills/briefing/references/gtd-rules.md
  • Calendar in Notion: "fixed appointments"; the briefing shows them outside the task limit. 11 cards: 9 with only a Reminder, 1 with only a Due Date, 1 with both dates, all with an Estimate, 4 with ♻️ Recurring. gtd-rules.md; query on the GTD database, 2026-11-27
  • In the last apply the user removed by hand the Reminder of a card where the AI had proposed Reminder = Due Date. ~/.ai/worklog/inbox/2026-11-27-0639.json and the pasted prompt
  • The briefing saves the day plan as a Reminder on the day; Today candidates are "only for the current days". gtd-rules.md

Out of scope

  • Times in dates (a Reminder with a time).
  • The briefing plan page and the briefing rules.
  • The AI rule "Reminder never in the past" stays; past dates only when picked by hand.

Decision log

What to do when the Reminder and the Due Date are on the same day?

Rule in processing.md: with a Due Date and no earlier Reminder, the Reminder stays empty. On the page, picking a Due Date equal to the Reminder clears the Reminder (with a message), and apply skips a Reminder equal to the Due Date. It cannot be set by mistake. Rule in processing.md, and the page shows a warning for equal dates without changing the fields. The decision stays with the user. Page and apply unchanged. The least code, but both dates can still be set to the same day by hand.

Only a rule in processing.md: with a Due Date the AI does not propose a Reminder on the same day. Page and apply unchanged; both dates can be set by hand.

Which fields does the Calendar status have on the page?

Category, Estimate, Priority, Reminder, Due Date, Project. Matches the existing cards (Estimate and dates). Category, Reminder, Due Date, Project, like Someday/Maybe. Simpler, but the existing cards have an Estimate.

Recommended option accepted: Calendar has the Next Actions fields (Category, Estimate, Priority, Reminder, Due Date, Project), like the existing cards.

When does the AI propose Calendar instead of Next Actions?

A fixed date (a visit, an installation, signing a contract) or something that only makes sense from a date (check something in December). Reminder = that day, required: without a date the card stays Next Actions. Narrow: only events with a specific day. Everything else with a date stays Next Actions with a Reminder. Calendar only by hand on the page.

Recommended option accepted: Calendar when something can only be done from a given day; Reminder = that day, without a date the card stays Next Actions.

How to pick a date on the page?

The list stays (today, tomorrow, next days), at the end "Pick date…" opens the browser calendar (input type="date", no min, so past dates too). The quick picks stay, the calendar for the rest. A date field instead of the list, next to it an "×" button to clear. One mechanism, but "tomorrow" is always two clicks in the calendar. A popup with a month grid and "today", "tomorrow", "+1 week", "none" buttons. The most convenient and consistent with the page look, but the most code.

The browser date field replaces the list, with an "×" button to clear; no min, so past dates too.

Add other missing statuses along the way?

The others are day planning states (briefing) or rare cases. Things to run around town. The card goes straight to today, bypassing the briefing. An entry that turns out to be a project.

Also Today Candidates

Three statuses are added: Calendar, Today and (from the note) Today candidates. The "only" in option A does not exclude C and the note: A means Calendar here. Fields and AI proposals for Today and Today candidates in D6 and D7.

Which fields do Today and Today candidates have?

The Next Actions fields; picking the status without a Reminder sets the Reminder to today (as Waiting for sets +3 days). The card also shows up in the Top view. The same fields, Reminder only by hand. The status itself says "today", the date is not needed.

Recommended option accepted: the Next Actions fields, and picking Today or Today candidates without a Reminder sets it to today.

Does the AI propose Today or Today candidates?

The briefing plans the day (limit of 4 tasks); the AI in the Inbox proposes Next Actions with a Reminder today, as now. When something blocks someone or is due today, the AI proposes Today candidates; the briefing picks from them. The AI sets Today straight away, bypassing the briefing.

Recommended option accepted: Today and Today candidates only by hand on the page.
