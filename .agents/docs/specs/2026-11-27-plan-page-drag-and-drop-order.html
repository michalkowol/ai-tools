Plan page: reorder items by drag and drop

On the day plan page the order of items can be changed by drag & drop.

Context

The user wants to be able to modify the plan lightly on the page: change the order by drag & drop.

Facts

  • The order of items on the page is the order of the lists in the plan JSON; the renderer sorts nothing. The order of the section cards is fixed in the code. plugins/daily-workflow/scripts/wl/plan.py render():186-241
  • Lists with items: priorities, outside_limit, code_review, my_prs, needs_attention, meetings, tomorrow_prep, follow_ups, resolved, notion_changes; plus the agenda points under a meeting and the lists in "For the daily" and "After the daily". plan.py LIST_SECTIONS, all_items
  • code_review is ordered (first = review first); the AI orders it with the rule "unblocking the team comes first". skills/morning/references/plan-schema.md:71; skills/morning/SKILL.md:40-41
  • Today's tasks are at most 4 tasks (1 deep focus, 1 heavy, 2 light); their order has no described meaning. references/gtd-rules.md:50
  • The item key is the section + the URL (or the title), not the place in the list, so ticks and notes survive a reorder. plan.py item_key; tests/test_worklog.py test_keys_survive_reordering
  • The page has no backend. Ticks and notes live in localStorage and get back through the /daily-workflow:morning apply <file> prompt; worklog plan-apply reads JSON lines with key and done or note. scripts/wl/assets/plan.js:33-38; scripts/worklog.py:195; plan.py apply_lines
  • update after the daily corrects priorities and code_review; it has to keep done, done_items and ai_notes. SKILL.md, "Update after the daily"; plan-schema.md
  • A note for AI can already move, remove or add an item in the plan, in free text. SKILL.md, "Notes for AI"
  • Plan pages have no JS dependencies; earlier specs excluded Alpine.js and other libraries. plan.js and plan.css are copied to plans/assets/ and work from file://. .agents/docs/specs/2026-11-24-briefing-plan-checkboxes.html and 2026-11-27-briefing-plan-page-redesign.html, Out of scope; plan.py copy_assets
  • Every item already has a checkbox on the left and a "+ note" button on the right, visible on hover. plan.js PlanItem; scripts/wl/assets/plan.css
  • The Notion database has no order field (fields: Name, Status, Category, Estimate, Tags, Project, Reminder, Due Date, URL). gtd-rules.md, "Fields"

Out of scope

  • Changes in Notion that follow from the order.
  • Editing the content of an item on the page (title, estimate); notes for AI serve that.
  • The Deep Work block and the three acts of the day.
  • The Inbox page.
  • Dragging an item to another section (D1) and reordering whole sections (D3).
  • Touch screens and a drag and drop library (D7).

Decision log

Where can an item be dragged?

Reordering inside one list, e.g. in Today's tasks or Code review order. The item does not change its section, so its fields stay unchanged. E.g. from Outside the limit to Today's tasks or from Today's tasks to Prep for tomorrow. Sections have different fields (kind, estimate, repo), so a move needs extra rules or the AI.

Recommended option accepted: an item is reordered only inside its own list. Moving it to another section stays possible through a note for AI.

Which lists can be sorted?

Today's tasks, Outside the limit, Code review order, My pull requests, Needs attention, Prep for tomorrow, Follow-ups. Not the agenda points, the "For the daily" and "After the daily" lists, Resolved and Notion changes. Also the agenda points, "For the daily", "After the daily", Resolved and Notion changes. The two lists where the order means the order of work.

Recommended option accepted: the seven task lists are sortable. Meetings are ordered by time, so they are not sorted in any variant.

Can whole sections be reordered too?

The order of the section cards is fixed, the same on every plan page. Section cards can be dragged too; their order then has to be stored separately.

Recommended option accepted: the order of the section cards stays fixed.

Where is the new order stored?

The page keeps the order in localStorage. The apply prompt carries it together with the ticks and notes, and worklog plan-apply reorders the lists in the plan JSON. The order is then visible to the AI and survives update. No prompt: the page restores the order every time it opens. The plan JSON does not know it, so the AI does not see it and update may arrange the list its own way.

Recommended option accepted. The page has no backend, so the order lives in the browser right away and reaches the plan JSON with the apply prompt, together with the ticks and notes.

Should update after the daily keep the manual order?

Items arranged by hand stay in that order; the AI puts new items where they fit and says in its answer what it changed. After the daily the AI arranges the lists again by its own rules (e.g. in reviews, unblocking the team first).

Recommended option accepted: update keeps the order the user arranged and only places new items.

What is the item dragged by?

A small handle visible on hover, like the "+ note" button. Selecting text and the links in the item work as before. A bigger target, but selecting text in the item gets harder and links are dragged as links.

Recommended option accepted: a handle shown on hover, so text selection and links keep working.

How to build the dragging?

No dependency, little code. Works with the mouse; does not work on touch screens, and the animation is simpler. Smoother animation and touch support, but a third-party library file is added to the plugin to maintain.

Recommended option accepted: plan pages stay without JS dependencies. In both variants the handle also works from the keyboard (arrow keys move the item).
